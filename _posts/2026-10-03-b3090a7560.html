This is the Ruscombe chapel graveyard....which is designated as a Space for Nature....where nature can flourish. Over the last 2 years, its been carefully looked after. ...with yellow rattle being planted there.....as the first step to cultivating lots of wild flowers to bloom and blossom there. Its recently been strimmed and raked....in preparation for lots of native wild flower seeds to be scattered there. The Parish Council have generously paid for lots of these seeds to be scattered in various locations in the village....including the chapel graveyard. Dont be surprised therefore if soon..you see people sowing and scattering...
