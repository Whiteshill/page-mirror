New Councillor wanted Good afternoon. Recently Kris Atkins stepped down from being a Councillor, and notices have been put up. No By-election has been called and therefore we can co-opt a new Councillor. If you are interested in being a Councillor for Whiteshill and Ruscombe Parish Council please do get in touch. This co-option can take place at the November meeting if we have candidates come forward. Nigel Pitel has also just stepped down from being a Councillor and notices are now out to see if a by-election is called for this position. If not then co-option can take place for this vacancy in December. Thanks to both Kris and Nigel for their work during their time as Cllrs Please get in touch with the Parish Clerk for more information in becoming a Cllr and representing your community
