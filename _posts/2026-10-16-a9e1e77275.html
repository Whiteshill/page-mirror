🌾 Calling All Scarecrow Makers! 🌾

The annual Scarecrow Trail is returning, and we're looking for local residents & families, to get involved!

Whether you're a crafting expert or simply fancy having some fun, we'd love you to create a scarecrow for this year's trail. The more scarecrows we have, the bigger and better the event will be.

✅ Open to everyone
✅ Great fun for all ages
✅ Show off your creativity
✅ Help bring our community together

Why not gather some old clothes, a few bits of straw, and let your imagination run wild?

To register your scarecrow or find out more, please contact Kate by Wednesday 21st October via mobile 07738 624492 or email friendsofwhiteshillschool@gmail.com

Let's fill the village with fantastic characters, colourful creations and plenty of smiles!
