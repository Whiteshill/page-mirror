How might climate change affect life in our community over the coming years? Following a recent screening in which Randwick Village Hall was at capacity, there will be a further showing of the **People's Emergency Briefing** at **St Paul’s Church Whiteshill, 7-9pm on Friday 27th November**.

The event includes a 50-minute ‘Gogglebox’ style film featuring **Chris Packham** and leading UK experts, followed by a short discussion around local activity and opportunities. **Admission is free, but please book a space here:** [https://tinyurl.com/WhiteshillPEB](https://tinyurl.com/WhiteshillPEB)

Please note that St Paul’s Church is unheated, so we do recommend dressing warmly on the night. There will be tea, coffee and biscuits available.

Do please share with friends and neighbours.
