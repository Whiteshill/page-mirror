We ate the Star Sunday Roast last w/e and .......

Bear in mind that the competition these days is very strong; we've been going to the Carpenters because it's a lovely experience, good food, & great value. The Vinetree has turned into a gastronomic paradise but ....

The Star beat all. The service was very good; the vegetable & gravy servings were great & generous, the Roast Turkey was reported to me as very good, but the Roast Beef was the best that I have ever had.

Well done to the new owners!
