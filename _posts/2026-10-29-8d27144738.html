What a lovely, sunny day it was today ! Plus so many people were out enjoying the Scarecrow Trail - right through till late afternoon !
We could hear the excited chatter from Ruscombe Chapel's SchoolRoom where Coffee Morning was in full flow.

So we were delighted to be visited by several families who popped in for drinks, biscuits & cakes ... including "bat poo cup cakes" (ok, I didn't actually spend last night collecting bat poo - substituted with currants), chocolate spider cup cakes, apple cup cakes topped with spooky Halloween sprinkles & shaped Halloween cakes) - they went down well with regulars & visitors alike.

The "resident dogs" - Eddie & Kerensa enjoyed meeting new people too ... and getting extra treats !

We ended up staying "open" later than usual !

So, many thanks to everyone who came along today. The donations for refreshments help towards the running costs of Ruscombe Chapel and SchoolRoom.

Reminder - no Coffee Morning next Thursday, 5th November, as it's LunchClub - serving up around 12.30pm, 2 course home-cooked lunch + tea/coffee - suggested donation £6
