I just wanted to say a huge thank you the fundraisers and organisers of the bike track. We went there with an 8 year old and 6 year old today and yesterday. It was very busy but all kids were respectful to one another and polite. They were so kind to my 6 year old and some of them even offered her tips.
There were a few minor crashes where the older kids checked on each other, made sure they were ok and carried on. All kids were having a great time. It's such a huge asset to the community. Thank you!
