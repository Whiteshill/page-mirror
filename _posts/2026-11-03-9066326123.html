The winners of Whiteshill Scarecrow Trail 2026 are...
🥇 1st place - Holly Walter with Derpy from K-Pop Demon Hunters (4) with 31 votes

🥈 2nd place - Laura Asplin with Mario Kart (9) with 24 votes

🥉 3rd place - Ralph Mundy with Wallace and Gromit rocket (27) with 21 votes

With such tough competition, there were some other scarecrows to call out as they were very close to the top:

Diary of a Wimpey Kid (26) with 19 votes

How to Train your Dragon (15) with 13 votes.

We've had another really excellent year with so many fantastic scarecrows and visitors to our lovely village. Thank you to everyone who spent hours (and in some cases weeks) making scarecrows, to the organisers who pulled it together and to everyone who bought a map and supported our wonderful school. A special thank you to Truhouse and SJR Carpets & Flooring as our two sponsors providing the prizes and money towards the trail. The event provides vital funds for the school, so every map sold or donation made means so much.

Our lucky map entry winner this year was Robyn Roberts!

Until next year!! See you again.

Friends of Whiteshill School Committee
