We all love the woods that surround Whiteshill and Ruscombe, especially in the spring when the bluebells are in bloom.

Did you know that 17 acres of this precious asset is up for sale?

Imagine how awful it would be if they were bought by someone who wanted to build on them....

And, equally, imagine how wonderful it would be if the village could own them, and pass them, safely, to future generatons....

The good news is that there is currently a project going on which seeks to do exactly that....

**10 November Panel Discussion and Q and A**

The next stage of this project is a Panel Discussion and Q and A session in the Ruscombe Schoolroom on 10 Nov, 1900 - 2030....where you can find out more, and ask those questions you may have.

## ***It would be good to see you there, if you can make it.***

**Pledging Support**

The project is also now seeking pledges from people. In order to show major funders how much the village wants to protect our woods, we need to demonstrate clear community backing. Securing local pledges will help us unlock matching grant funding.

*A pledge is a non-binding expression of support to show that you would be willing to contribute if the purchase goes ahead. No money is collected at this stage and it carries no risk.*

If you're interested in making a pledge, you'll be able to make a pledge directly to:

[www.ruscombe-woods.org.uk](www.ruscombe-woods.org.uk)

Thanks for taking the time to read this..
