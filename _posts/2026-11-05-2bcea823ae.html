**⚡ Calling Local Electricians! Can you lend a hand to Paganhill Community Group? ⚡**

We are expanding our community hub at **The Octagon** and setting up our new **Men's Shed** container right next to the building! To get it up and running as a safe, welcoming workshop and gathering space for the local community, we need to get power connected.

**Here is where we need your help:**
* 🔌 **The Job:** Installing and connecting electricity to the Men's Shed container.
* 📦 **Materials:** All necessary electrical supplies and equipment are **already provided on-site**.
* 📜 **Who we're looking for:** A certified / qualified electrician willing to donate a bit of time and expertise to a great community cause.

By volunteering your skills, you will be making a direct impact on local wellbeing, combating loneliness, and giving our community a fantastic space to build and connect.

💬 **Interested or know someone who might be?** Send us a direct message here on Facebook/Instagram or email us at: **hello@paganhill.org.uk**

*Please share this post with friends, family, and local trade groups in and around Stroud!*
