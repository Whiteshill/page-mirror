Please come together at 10.30am this Remembrance Sunday November 8th at the War Memorial to stand and remember those in our Parish who lost their lives in the 1st and 2nd World Wars. We will then walk down to St Paul's for the Service of Remembrance starting at 11.00am. There will be a two-minute silence followed by the Service led by Rev Coral Francis. Refreshments will be served afterwards.
