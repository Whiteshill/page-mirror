**Stroud Local History Society**** talk on ****The General Strike in 1926 with Stuart Butler – Thursday 19****th**** November Shambles Hall**

The centenary of the General Strike this year has been marked by a number of events all over the country.

Viewed by the government and its supporters as a threat to parliamentary government and the constitution, how did this unique event play out in Stroud & District?

The TUC and its supporters saw this unique strike as an industrial not political struggle. How did it play out along the railway between Stroud and Swindon?

Stuart Butler (who has co-written a pamphlet about the General Strike in Stroud and Gloucester with Tony Condor) exhibited, spoke, walked and talked about the General Strike in May this year - he will inform and entertain us in his talk.
