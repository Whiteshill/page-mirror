We've had an amazing response from craft stalls already (thank you!), so we're now really looking to add more **food and drink producers** to our **Sunflowers x The Old Crown Festive Market**.

We're especially keen to hear from:
🧀 Artisan food producers
🍫 Chocolatiers & sweet treats
☕ Speciality tea, coffee & drinks
🎁 Festive foodie gifts
🥖 Local produce & farm shop suppliers

We're still happy to hear from craft stallholders too, but food stalls are what we're most looking for right now.

🚫 No hot food vendors or bar providers needed.

If you'd like a stall at a friendly community Christmas market supporting local charity, Sunflowers Suicide Support, we'd love to hear from you!

📩 Drop us a message for more information or to book your space.

Please tag your favourite local food businesses or give this a share to help us spread the word!
