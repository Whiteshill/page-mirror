A reminder that we're hosting the **People's Emergency Briefing** at **St Paul's Church, Friday 27 November (7-9pm)**.

The evening starts with a 50-minute film featuring leading UK experts, followed by a discussion about what the issues raised might mean for us locally and how we might strengthen local resilience and preparedness for the future.

We're particularly interested in hearing about existing local projects, community initiatives, and new ideas that could help make our area more connected, resilient and prepared. We'll also have displays showing the great initiatives already going on.

There is still plenty of capacity, so we'd love to welcome more neighbours.

Tea and coffee provided. Please dress warmly as the church is unheated.
