Shout out to Ruscombe residents ..... The Ruscombe Advent Window Trail needs you !!!
Would you like to be part of this year's Ruscombe Advent Window Trail ?

The idea is you create a design in a "window" that you illuminate each evening starting on the date you get allocated.

Tip .. use a timer on a lamp / fairy lights with inbuilt timers .. that way you can preset the on /off times for the window to be illuminated.

Let's make it a fab Trail this year !
