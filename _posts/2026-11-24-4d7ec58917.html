Patsy Burns
Hello

I'd just like to take this opportunity to let anyone who knew Patsy,

she sadly died on 11th November.

She was part of Whiteshill village for many years, over 25 at least.

You maybe knew her from being in the village,catching the bus or out and about in Stroud.

Her family would like anyone who knew Patsy in whatever capacity, to know they are welcome to attend her funeral and a celebration of her life.

Her burial funeral will take place St Paul's Whiteshill.

Please make sure you arrive wrapped up and warm as it is chilly in the church at this time of year.

Date:

Tuesday 8th December at noon,

followed by refreshments at The Star pub in Whiteshill.
