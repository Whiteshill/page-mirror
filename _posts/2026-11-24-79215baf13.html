📣New location 🚨
Following the fantastic turnout at our last Lift the Curfew run, we’ve made a small change to our meeting point for 8 December.

📍 NEW MEET PLACE: Outside Woody’s, Cainscross

Archway is busy with its sports facilities, so we’re moving to make parking easier. There is plenty of parking in the public car park behind Woody’s.

⏰ Arrive from 6pm – aiming to leave at 6:15pm.

🏃‍♀️ Club members: Please book onto the run through the club app.

🏃‍♀️ Non-club members: Please sign in with us when you arrive, providing your name and ICE (in case of emergency) details.

It’s still the same FREE, festive 5K, taking in the Christmas lights, with plenty of fun and good vibes! All abilities welcome and nobody gets left behind. 🎅🏼

PLEASE SPREAD THE WORD ABOUT OUR NEW MEET PLACE! ❤️💚

See you there! 🎄
