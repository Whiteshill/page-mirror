Over the past 6 months, our three directors and the whole team have worked incredibly hard to make The Star Inn a true community pub — somewhere people can come together, catch up with friends, meet new faces and feel part of something.

It's been wonderful seeing the pub become a place for the community, and we'd love to finish the year by celebrating with all of you. 🍾⭐🥂⭐

A huge thank you to everyone who continues to support us. Whether you pop in for a drink, enjoy a meal, come along to our events or simply spread the word, it really does mean a lot to us. The Star wouldn't be what it is without you ⭐

Join us on Thursday 31st December for a fantastic evening of music with DJ Alex, a delicious Greek buffet, and a glass of Bouvet on arrival and again at midnight.
